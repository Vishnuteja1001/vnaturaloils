Our Heritage

Generations of purity and honesty.

At V Natural Oils, our journey is deeply personal. Production began with my grandfather, whose unwavering honesty and dedication to supplying truly healthy, pure oils to his customers built the foundation of our family's legacy.

Inspired by his values and the sacred symbol of Sri Venkateswara Swamy, I started this company to extend his incredible legacy to more homes. Today, we continue to honor his vision by bringing you the finest cold-pressed Castor, Neem, and Groundnut oils.
